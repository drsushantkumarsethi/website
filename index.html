Dr. Sushant Kumar Sethi is a Senior Consultant in Gastroenterology and Hepatology based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, with over 25 years of medical experience.

His clinical background includes AIIMS, New Delhi, Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals, New Delhi, and IMS & SUM Hospitals, Bhubaneswar. As Head of Academics, he also contributes to specialist training, research, and professional learning.

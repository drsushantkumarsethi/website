Patient Orientation & Guidance

Patient Resources & Clinical Information

Comprehensive guidelines to help you prepare for your consultation, understand procedure protocols, and access evidence-based gastrointestinal health information.

Urgent Care & Acute Emergency Advisory

Scheduled outpatient consultations are intended for elective, non-emergency evaluations. If you or a loved one experiences any of the following acute symptoms, seek immediate emergency care at the nearest hospital emergency department:

Vomiting blood (hematemesis) or dark brown coffee-ground material
Black, tarry bowel movements (melena) or heavy rectal bleeding
Sudden, acute, severe abdominal pain or abdominal rigidity
Rapidly progressive jaundice accompanied by mental confusion or high fever
Essential Preparation

First Consultation Checklist

What to bring and how to prepare for an effective gastrointestinal or hepatology consultation.

Complete list of current prescription medicines, painkillers, and over-the-counter supplements.
Prior endoscopy, colonoscopy, and ultrasound imaging reports with films or discs if available.
Recent blood test results including liver function tests (LFT), CBC, and viral serologies.
Chronological timeline of symptoms, dietary triggers, and prior treatments tried.
Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete clinical registration.
Diagnostic Procedure

Upper GI Endoscopy Preparation

General guidelines for scheduled diagnostic upper gastrointestinal endoscopy.

Maintain fasting (no solid foods) for at least 6 to 8 hours prior to the procedure.
Clear sips of water may be permitted up to 2 hours before the procedure unless advised otherwise.
Inform Dr. Sethi about any blood thinners, antiplatelet medicines, or diabetes medications.
Arrange for a family member or attendant to accompany you if sedation is planned.
Individual preparation instructions will be provided and confirmed during your pre-procedure visit.
Screening Procedure

Colonoscopy Preparation & Bowel Cleansing

Essential preparation protocols for diagnostic or screening colonoscopy examinations.

Follow a low-fiber diet starting 2 to 3 days before the examination.
Consume only clear liquids on the day prior to the procedure as instructed.
Complete the prescribed split-dose bowel cleansing solution strictly according to timing instructions.
Arrange for an escort to drive you home following the procedure.
Proper bowel preparation is critical for accurate mucosal visualization and polyp detection.

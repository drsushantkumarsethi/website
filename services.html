Doctor-Approved Clinical Consultation Guidance

Every patient’s gastrointestinal health requires a thorough, personalized diagnostic strategy.

When is specialized evaluation recommended?

  • Symptoms persistent for more than 2-3 weeks despite basic medication.
  • Noticeable unintentional weight loss or poor appetite.
  • Elevated liver enzymes on routine annual blood tests.
  • Recurrent heartburn, regurgitation, or swallowing discomfort.

How to maximize your consultation:

  • Bring chronological copies of previous ultrasounds, CT scans, and blood tests.
  • List any regular medications (including pain relievers and antacids).
  • Bring prior endoscopy or colonoscopy reports with biopsy results if available.

01 / UNDERSTANDING THE CONDITION

What is crohn’s disease?

Crohn’s disease is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) that causes inflammation of the digestive tract lining, most commonly affecting the small intestine and colon.

02 / RECOGNISING THE PATTERN

Symptoms to discuss.

Persistent diarrhea, often accompanied by cramping abdominal pain
Unexplained weight loss and reduced appetite
Fatigue and low-grade fever
Blood in the stool or anal fissures/fistulas
03 / FINDING CLARITY

How it may be assessed.

Comprehensive evaluation incorporates ileocolonoscopy with biopsy, inflammatory blood markers (CRP, ESR), fecal calprotectin, and cross-sectional imaging.

04 / YOUR WAY FORWARD

A personal approach to care.

Long-term management focuses on mucosal healing and remission using immunomodulators, biologics, and anti-inflammatory therapies alongside nutritional optimization.

Before your consultation
Bring all previous colonoscopy reports, pathology biopsy slides/impressions, and CT/MRI enterography scans.

When symptoms need urgent attention
Seek urgent medical care for severe or worsening abdominal pain, vomiting blood, black stools, fainting, or chest pain. Do not wait for a routine consultation if you feel acutely unwell.
